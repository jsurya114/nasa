Privacy Policy

NASA Logistics Carriers LLC operates NASA Routes to manage delivery work, driver accounts, payments, and accounting records.

Last updated November 12, 2026

Information we handle

We handle account and contact information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, and sign-in information. We also handle information you or an authorized administrator provide, including driver agreements, availability, delivery and route records, carrier reports, claims, earnings, deductions, invoices, and payout records.

NASA Routes uses session information, browser or device storage, and service logs to support sign-in, remember preferences, and troubleshoot the service. The mobile app may use a device token to deliver notifications when enabled.

How we use and share information

We use information to operate the service, manage delivery work, verify and administer accounts, prepare statements and payments, keep accounting records, respond to support requests, and protect the service. Authorized NASA staff can access information relevant to their work. We share information as needed with providers that support hosting, notifications, payments, or accounting, and when required by law.

Everee and QuickBooks Online

For drivers paid through Everee, Everee collects banking and tax details on its own onboarding screens. NASA Routes uses the information needed to link the driver to Everee, send payment instructions, and display payment and onboarding status.

When an authorized administrator connects QuickBooks Online, NASA Routes uses the connected company’s account and vendor references and exchanges vendor details, bills, and payment records to record driver payments already made outside QuickBooks. When a driver submits a tax ID during QuickBooks setup, NASA Routes passes it to QuickBooks for vendor setup. NASA Routes does not store that tax ID in its database.

NASA Logistics Carriers LLC handles this information for the purposes described here. Intuit and Everee handle information in their own services under their own privacy policies. A company administrator can revoke QuickBooks access through Intuit or contact us for help disconnecting.

Retention and requests

We keep records as needed to operate NASA Routes, resolve disputes, and meet applicable accounting, tax, and other recordkeeping obligations. Disconnecting an integration does not by itself erase records already created.

Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of your information. We may verify your identity before responding. Some records may need to be retained to meet applicable obligations.
