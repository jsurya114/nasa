**What the driver gets (Pay page).**
- **Everee transfers:** the driver's own transfers, from NASA's records only (`GET /driver/everee/transfers`: the driver is the token's, nothing in the URL or query, `no-store`, nothing asked of Everee, at most 50, newest first). Each shows the exact decimal amount, the period it pays, when it was sent, when NASA last checked and, for a verified one, when it was deposited. The five statuses are **never merged**: With Everee (pending), Not confirmed (unconfirmed), Not sent (failed), Returned by bank, Deposited (completed). "Deposited" appears **only** when NASA verified Everee's payout as paid (a returned deposit is shown as Returned even if it was paid before); a pending transfer says the money has not reached the bank yet; an unconfirmed one says not to count it as paid. A short note says a transfer pays one of the approved statements above (same money, not extra) and that cash payments are not listed here, so earned pay, deductions, cash receipts and Everee transfers are labelled apart and never added together.
- **Payment history and Bank details:** two buttons, only when the server says the driver's setup is complete. Each opens Everee's own `PAYMENT_HISTORY` or `PAYMENT_DEPOSIT` screen with a fresh single-use link, exactly like the setup (web: iframe with a private channel; phone app: the shell's separate window). NASA never sees bank details. Closing a window reads the transfers again. The server's refusals (not complete, too many attempts, unavailable) are shown in words. They reuse A's self-only session route, which already refuses these screens until the driver is complete.
- **History stays readable.** The transfers list does not depend on the driver's current type or setup state: a driver changed to Desi, or one whose setup is incomplete, still reads their earlier transfers. Only the two Everee-screen buttons need a complete setup.
- English and Spanish for every text.

**A decision for the reviewers: no native API proxy.** Jasil's note allows proxying Everee's worker payment-history and pay-stub endpoints "if NASA needs native list/PDF controls". I did **not** build it: Everee's own history screen already lists payments and pay stubs and opens the documents, NASA's transfers list covers what NASA itself verified, and a proxy would add a new place that handles expiring document links and rate limits (25 requests per 10 seconds) for little gain. If the team wants native lists or PDF buttons, it is a separate, small PR on top of this. Worker history in Everee appears only after processing for deposit and is not a complete pending/failed list or proof of settlement; this list is therefore NASA's, and says so.

- **Database:** None. **Config and dependencies:** none. **Money impact:** None. Nothing writes; the read does not touch payments, statements or flags.
