**What changed** (each point is a finding in Jasil's review).
1. **Everee in the shared Payment history (P1).** `GET .../payments/runs/:runId` (`reviewRunPayments`) now adds, for each statement that has an Everee payment item, a `providerPayment` object beside the unchanged manual `payment` field: `provider` (`everee`), `systemVerified` (true only when Everee confirmed the deposit), `batchId`, `itemId`, `environment`, `status`, `providerReference` (Everee's payment ID), `paidOn`, `confirmedAt`, `requestedByName`, `initiatedAt`, `lastCheckedAt`, `returned`, `needsReview`, `note`. Only a super admin (who may already see the Everee batch view) gets all of these; any other admin who may open the run's history gets the outcome only (`provider`, `systemVerified`, `environment`, `status`, `paidOn`, `returned`, `needsReview`), with no IDs, references, requester names or notes (found by the automated security review of the commit). It is `null` for a statement with no Everee payment, and empty when the Everee tables are not installed, so the manual flow cannot break. The Payment records window (opened from the Dashboard card and from the approved-run detail, including after the whole run is paid) shows it under the driver, labelled as verified by the system, not as an admin's manual receipt, with a clear line for a returned deposit and for sandbox ("no real money"). The manual receipt fields and their rendering are unchanged.
2. **Disclosures before confirming (P1).** The confirm screen now shows, above the driver list and before the confirm button, the unresolved city claims (`summary.pendingClaims`, "not deducted in these payments") and the number of drivers with warnings, and under each eligible driver the server's disclose-level lines (for example an earlier failed payment to check). It is warn-only: the confirm button is not disabled by them.
3. **The window refreshes (P2).** While any payment of the run is waiting for Everee, the open window re-reads the server's results every minute (a read only; a failed read keeps the last results and tries again). When a payment becomes paid it tells the page behind it. The text now says what is true: Everee is checked every 15 minutes by the server, this window refreshes every minute, or press Check now.

**Database:** none. **Config and dependencies:** none. **Money impact:** none; the added query only reads.

**Tested.** Backend (rolled back, fake Everee): the history shows the Everee payment as verified by the system with reference, pay date, requester and environment, a sent-but-unpaid one is not verified, a city admin gets the reduced view, and the manual receipt field stays `null`/unchanged (manual payment tests: 6 pass). Frontend (made-up answers, 10 Everee tests): warnings and claims are visible before the confirm button and do not disable it; the window shows a payment turning paid by itself and notifies the page, then offers no Check now; the history shows the Everee card (reference, pay date, requester, returned deposit, sandbox, verified by the system) for the Everee driver only and the manual receipt as before. Full frontend suite: 241 pass, build and lint on the changed files clean.

**Not tested.** A real sandbox payment reaching `PAID` and its history in a real browser (no finalized run with an Everee payment exists locally; the client's funding account is still missing).

**Risks and follow-ups.** The `providerPayment` shape is the contract Jasil proposed; if he wants other names, only the one backend function and the one component change. The refresh is a plain one-minute poll of the batch view, one request pair per open window.
