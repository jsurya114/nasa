# Insights: claim losses and profit after them

The carrier charges NASA for claims, and NASA takes them back from drivers in later pay runs. A claim that is never taken back (the driver stopped working, no driver could be matched, or the claim is exempt by agreement) is money the company is out, but Insights showed only the approved delivery margin, which is before claims. Insights now has a **Claim losses and profit** section under the headline figures: the delivery margin (unchanged, "gross profit before overheads"), the claim losses, and **profit after claim losses**, with a breakdown.

What counts, decided with the owner: every claim not yet deducted from a driver counts at once (a claim of an active driver still reduces profit until it is taken). The breakdown says how much of it is "likely lost" (the driver is disabled or has not delivered for 30 days) and how much is "still recoverable". Also counted: claims no driver is matched to, claims NASA absorbs by agreement (DFW GOFO + Tracking interruption), and driver insurance not recovered (the carrier's Driver Service Fee on each published statement, less the insurance withheld from that city's drivers in that billing week; only a shortfall is a loss, a surplus is shown and not counted as a gain). Claims count by their own date inside the period, recovery is as of now, and a claim the carrier refunded is already netted out of the claim, so it is never counted.

The approved margin definition is not changed. Two definitions are added (`claim_losses`, `profit_after_claim_losses`, definitions version 2026-11-10), so the Insights page and the Claude connector describe them the same way. New endpoint `GET /admin/insights/losses` (super admin only, read only, same filters as the other reports). New migration `2026-11-10-reporting-claim-losses.sql` (two read-only reporting views; additive, with a rollback). Claim losses are not split by daily or weekly source; with a source filter on, the margin is for that source and the losses are for all, and the section says so.

Not included, on purpose: other carrier fees (withdrawal fees and the carrier's other ledger adjustments, such as a DSP Warehouse Violation Penalty, which appears in the supplied workbooks), and driver debts (advances and manual penalties). Both are possible follow-ups.

Validation: a database test with made-up 2090 data (unrecovered split, a draft run not counting, an older claim version and a claim before the period left out, insurance shortfall and surplus, only the Driver Service Fee counted, margin less losses, nothing changed by reading); API tests for the new route; 4 Insights page tests; whole frontend suite (557) and backend suite (300, the database ones with MCP_TEST_DB=1) pass. Run against the local database for Sep-Nov 2026 the report gives 520.00 of losses on 174,265.49 of margin; there are no carrier statements locally, so the insurance line was checked with made-up statements only.
