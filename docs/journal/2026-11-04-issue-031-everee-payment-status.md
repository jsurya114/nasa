**What changed.** An Everee item becomes `paid` in exactly one place, and only when Everee's Payment Data API (`GET /integration/v1/workers/payment-history/{paymentId}`) says **every payout of the payment is `PAID`**. Sending a payable, an accepted payout request, a payable status of `PAID` and a webhook that says "paid" never do it; the answer is always read from Everee again.
- **Looking at an item** (`reconcileItem`): read the payable (its status and its payment ID). A blocked status (`ERROR`, unpayable worker, deleted) makes a submitted item `unconfirmed`; a status that means Everee is processing it moves an `unconfirmed` item back to `submitted`. With a payment ID, read the payment: not found yet (404) or `PENDING`: wait. Any payout `FAILED` (a bank returned the deposit): flag `deposit_returned`, never paid. Anything not understood: a note, never paid. All `PAID`: complete.
- **Completing** takes the same locks as manual recording, in the same order (run, city, statement, then the source rows), and calls `settlePaidStatements`, the function extracted from `recordRunPayment`. The statement, the earning rows it included and the item become paid in one transaction, once. The frozen net, deductions and debt are not touched. If the included earnings changed after approval, the money still landed so the statement is paid, but the changed rows are left as they are and the item note says to review the run. If Everee states the payout total and it differs from what was sent, the item is not marked paid and the note says so. The pay date is Everee's, else today's date in the city's time zone (default America/Chicago), not the server's.
- **A deposit returned after `paid`** cannot un-pay history. The item is flagged `deposit_returned` for a person to look at, and the flag clears when the retry settles. No webhook says a retry settled, so the check looks again.
- **Events:** `payment.paid`, `payment-payables.status-changed`, `payment.deposit-returned` and `payment.deposit-retried` pick the items they may concern (by payable ID, payment ID or worker) and look at them again, at most 10 per event inside Everee's short wait; a failure answers 500 so Everee redelivers. An event for a payment that is not ours does nothing.
- **Polling cadence: every 15 minutes** (`utils/evereeStatusJob.js`, `node-cron`), only while `EVEREE_PAYMENTS_ENABLED` is `true`, at most 40 items per run, about 120 ms apart (Everee allows 100 operations per 5 seconds). It looks at items that are submitted or unconfirmed and were asked for payout, and at paid items with a returned deposit, and skips items looked at in the last 15 minutes. A run that is still going is not started twice.
- **Check now:** `POST /admin/payroll/everee-batches/:batchId/check` (super admin of the batch's city) does the same for one batch. The batch view now also shows when an item was last checked, a note, the pay date and the paid time.

**Database** (`2026-11-04-issue-031-everee-payment-status.sql`, additive, re-runnable; back up and run on a copy first). Three columns on `everee_payment_items`: `everee_pay_date`, `last_checked_at`, `check_note` (300 characters at most). The guard function is re-created so those three may change like the other "what Everee said" columns; who is paid how much still cannot. The rollback drops them and restores the guard, and refuses if any item has a pay date. Applied to the local copy only.

- **Dependencies:** none (`node-cron` is already used). **Config:** none new.
- **Money impact:** this is the code that marks a statement paid for an Everee payment, with the same effect on the rows as recording by hand. No amount, deduction or debt is recalculated; the sandbox moves no real money.

**Tested.** Pure (4): all payouts `PAID` is landed, `PENDING` is pending, `FAILED` is returned and never hidden, and anything not understood is unknown. Database with a fake Everee, rolled back (13 steps): no payment yet, not processed yet (404), pending, an answer not understood and an unreadable payable all change nothing; a returned deposit and a total that differs are never paid; a landed payment pays the statement, the item and only that driver's rows once (frozen net unchanged, looking again changes nothing); manual recording cannot pay a statement Everee paid or one it is processing; a deposit returned after paid is flagged and clears on settle; a blocked payable goes `unconfirmed` and back to `submitted`, and an unconfirmed item that landed is paid; an event claiming "paid" while Everee says pending pays nothing; a signed `payment.paid` through the webhook pays once and the repeat is a duplicate; changed earnings; check now permissions; the scheduled check's switch and interval; the guard. Treating `PENDING` as landed makes 9 tests fail, ignoring a returned payout makes 6, dropping the amount check makes 3 and flipping changed rows makes 2 (checked). Backend suite: 333 pass, 4 fail (the same 4 claims-store tests fail on `develop` without this change).

**Sandbox evidence so far.** After the earlier batch, Everee emailed that a payroll run of $6.05 (the three test payables plus five one-cent probes) was ready for review and approval in the portal, so Everee did take the payables even though the status read `ERROR` at the time. Reading those payables by ID now answers 401 with the same token that still reads pay codes, work locations and workers, so a payable that is already in an approval run may not be readable. This code therefore never depends on reading a payable: a 401 or any failed read is a note, and the item keeps its status. The run still needs approving, and Everee asked for a funding account first; **no payment has reached `PAID` in the sandbox yet.**

**Not tested.** The real shape of the payment history answer (the docs show only `netPayouts` with a `status` for each payout; the payout amount field name and the pay date field are guesses that, if wrong, leave the amount check and pay date unused and never mark anything paid wrongly); a real webhook for a payment event; the payable's `paymentId` field name. All of this needs one real sandbox payment to reach `PAID` after the portal approval. No screen yet.

**Risks and follow-ups.** The 15 minute check runs in every server instance; it is safe (row locks and a once-only completion), only wasteful. The dashboard Pay button with the preview and confirm screens, and showing the Everee result next to manual receipts in Payment history, are the last part.
