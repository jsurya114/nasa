## Description

NASA Routes is the work app for NASA Logistics Carriers' drivers and administrators. You need an account created by NASA to sign in.

Drivers: record your day's journey and deliveries, set your weekly availability, see your deliveries and pay statements, send access-code photos for gated addresses, sign your driver agreement and finish payment setup with NASA's payroll providers.

Administrators: manage drivers, routes and cities, upload carrier reports, review journeys, availability, access codes and claims, prepare pay runs, and save or share invoices and pay-run PDFs from your iPhone.

Notifications remind you about availability, pay updates and agreements. You can request deletion of your account in the app.
